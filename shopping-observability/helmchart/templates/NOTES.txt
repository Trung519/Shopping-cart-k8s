Shopping Observability is configured for lab-k8s.

Retention:
  Loki keeps logs for 48h.

Check pods:
  kubectl --context k3d-lab-k8s -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} get pods,pvc,svc -o wide

If the namespace does not exist yet:
  kubectl --context k3d-lab-k8s create namespace {{ .Values.namespace.name }}

Open Grafana:
  kubectl --context k3d-lab-k8s -n {{ .Values.namespace.name }} port-forward svc/grafana 3000:80

Grafana:
  http://localhost:3000
  username: {{ .Values.grafana.adminUser }}
